Pod Garden: Privacy Policy

This game does not collect your personal information. It has no ads and no analytics tools that track what you do.

Saved game

Stored on this device and in your own iCloud account. The developer cannot see it.

Photos

Album photos stay on this device. They are saved or shared only when you choose to.

Notifications

Created on your device and delivered only if you turn them on. Nothing is sent elsewhere.

Purchases

All purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never receives your card details.

Contact

Use the support link on the App Store page. What you send is used only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
